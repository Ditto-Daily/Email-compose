### Why haven't you used affron branded saffron

### Why haven't you for vitamin k2 as it needs to be taken with vitamin D
I hope you’re well and so excited that you’re interested in DITTO! 

It's actually a common misconception that Vitamin K2 is needed for vitamin D absorption 😊.  Pairing them would provide no benefit unless you're specifically looking to target bone health. I will explain!
1. Vitamin D absorbs beautifully on its own! You'll often see D3 and K2 paired together, but that's because they work as a team after absorption - specifically for bone health, where they collaborate in calcium metabolism. One isn't actually necessary for absorbing the other. Since DITTO isn't formulated for bone health, including K2 would mean adding an ingredient that isn't directing its effects where we need them, and we'd rather keep every ingredient designed to target menstrual cycle-related mechanisms and pathways.

2. Some sources suggest K2 is needed to prevent vitamin D from causing calcium buildup in the blood, but this has only been shown to potentially happen at excessive doses well above the "safe upper limit". The safe upper limit for vitamin D is 100 micrograms (4,000 IU) - that's 6-7 times higher than what's in DITTO! So there's absolutely no risk unless your other supplements take you above this threshold.

I hope this helps clarify things! 🙂

### Why am i getting these funny tasting burps
The burp-back you mention can happen for some people when taking oil-containing supplements. The flavour you're noticing might taste a bit like tea or flowers as it comes from the chamomile oil. 

Here's what's happening: imagine a bottle of salad dressing before you shake it - the oil and water naturally separate. Similarly, when oil from the supplement is released in the stomach, it floats to the surface of the stomach contents and can sometimes cause that burp-back taste. 

If it bothers you, you could try taking the capsules at night time instead (this timing wont impact effectiveness of the supplement). Most customers also note that the burps go after a couple weeks of use.
